04 Manifesto

How we build, and why it ships.

→ 01Ship beats demo.

We measure ourselves by what survives contact with production — not by what shines in a slide deck. Every engagement ends with a system you run, not a prototype you maintain.

→ 02Domain over generalism.

An AI tool that knows nothing about your business is just a chatbot. We embed with your operators — pilots, lawyers, dispatchers — until we know the vocabulary, the edge cases, and the workflow.

→ 03Audit, evals, and observability — by default.

Every model decision is logged, every output is evaluated, every regression is caught before your users feel it. Trust is engineered, not promised.

→ 04Beautiful where it matters.

Operator-grade interfaces deserve craft. A clean dashboard isn't decoration — it's the difference between a tool that gets opened daily and one that gets abandoned in three weeks.

→ 05Own the system you get.

You leave the engagement with documentation, runbooks, and the ability to extend without us. We're a delivery partner, not a dependency.
